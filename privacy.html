Mittari Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Mittari does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Period.

  • No data collection — the app has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No network access — Mittari makes no network connections of any kind. Everything happens on your device.
  • No accounts — there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • Local storage only — your settings (counting mode, start values, goals, alert preference) and your current count are saved only in the app’s local storage on your device, purely so the app remembers them between launches. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Because Mittari collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell, delete, or request a copy of.
