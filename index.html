Feels amazing

Designed around the click.

  • The whole screen is the button — you never miss.
  • Crisp haptic feedback on every tap, with a stronger pulse every ten.
  • Digits roll up and down like a physical tally clicker.
  • Ripples follow your fingertip across the screen.
  • Goal reached? The whole screen changes color, and Mittari vibrates or plays a sound.
  • Swipe up to reset, like flicking to the next video.
  • Everything is remembered — mode, values, and your current count.
  • Beautiful in light and dark, on every device.

On your wrist

A real Apple Watch app.

Raise your wrist, tap the screen, and count with crisp watch haptics. It works standalone — no iPhone needed.

Private by design

No accounts. No ads. No network.

Mittari collects nothing and connects to nothing. Your counts live on your device and nowhere else.
